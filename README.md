# Applied Machine Learning
This is CS5785 at Cornell Tech
Cross-listed as ORIE5750 and ECE5414

Learn and apply key concepts of modelling, analysis and validation from machine learning, data mining and signal processing to analyze and extract meaning from data. Implement algorithms and perform experiments on images, text, audio and mobile sensor measurements. Gain working knowledge of supervised and unsupervised techniques including classification, regression, clustering, feature selection, and dimensionality reduction.

Prerequisites CS 2800 or equivalent and basic familiarity with Matlab or Python, or permission of instructor.

## Schedule:

### 1. Foundations (classes 1, 2, Python review)

Course overview and the supervised learning setting.
Loss functions and optimisation as the common frame for all later models.
Review session in Python.

### 2. Regression (classes 3, 4)

Linear regression, fitted by gradient descent.
Locally weighted regression and non-linear models.
Maximum likelihood as the statistical justification of the loss.

### 3. Classification (classes 5, 7, 9)

Logistic regression, softmax and Newton’s method.
Gaussian discriminant analysis and naive Bayes as generative classifiers.
The cost of misclassification, with false positives and false negatives.

### 4. Model design and data preparation (classes 6, 8)

Feature selection, regularisation and Monte Carlo methods.
Data set splits, overfitting, underfitting, and the bias and variance trade-off.
Data cleaning, preprocessing and the basics of text analysis.

### 5. Support vector machines (classes 9, 10)

The maximum margin classifier.
Kernel methods, and the kernel SVM for non-linear boundaries.

### 6. Trees and ensembles (class 11)

Decision and regression trees.
Ensemble methods, with bagging to reduce variance and boosting to reduce bias.

### 7. Neural networks (classes 12 to 14)

Optimisation algorithms and tricks: SGD, momentum, dropout, weight decay, Adagrad and Adam.
Neural Networks I, covering the perceptron as logistic regression, softmax, the multilayer perceptron, feed forward, and backpropagation with its computational aspects.
Neural Networks II, covering CNNs, ResNets and modern architectures.

### 8. Unsupervised learning (classes 15 to 17)

Clustering with k-means, mixture models and EM.
Dimensionality reduction with PCA and non-linear embeddings such as t-SNE and UMAP.
Representation learning with autoencoders, denoising autoencoders, and self-supervised and contrastive learning.

### 9. Modern generative models (classes 18 to 20)

Overview of generative models, and attention.
Language models, continued over a second class.
Diffusion models.

### 10. Data, evaluation and model improvement (classes 21 to 23)

Data issues such as collection, preprocessing, missing data, leakage, sampling bias and label quality.
Evaluating models and diagnosing their performance.
Model iteration, covering hyperparameter optimisation, transfer learning and fine-tuning.

### 11. Reinforcement learning and case study (classes 24, 27)

Reinforcement learning.
A case study of an open-source model, with training and setup.

### 12. Projects (classes 25, 26)

Poster presentations.
